PhoneGate changes how Windows signs you in. Please read this before you continue.

WHAT GETS INSTALLED
- The PhoneGate service, which holds this PC's key in its security chip (TPM).
- The PhoneGate sign-in tile, used on the lock screen and at sign-in.
- A watchdog that repairs PhoneGate if it is stopped or removed, and tells your phone.
- The PhoneGate app for this PC, and the PhoneGate app for your Android phone (to copy over).

PROTECTION STAYS OFF FOR NOW
Nothing changes at the lock screen yet. You'll turn protection on in the PhoneGate app,
after you pair your phone and save your recovery codes.

BEFORE YOU TURN PROTECTION ON
- If you can, try PhoneGate on a spare PC or a virtual machine first.
- Keep your 10 recovery codes somewhere safe, away from this PC. They are how you
  get in if your phone is lost or offline.
- Turn on BitLocker with a startup PIN (PhoneGate can help). Without it, someone
  with physical access could remove PhoneGate from the disk.
- You'll need your own PhoneGate relay server address (see the README).

This setup isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning.
Only run a setup file whose SHA-256 matches the one published with the release.
