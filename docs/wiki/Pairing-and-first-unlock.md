# Pairing and first unlock

1. On the PC, open **PhoneGate → Set up**:
   - **Desk details**: your relay address (for example `https://relay.example.com`, see
     [Hosting the relay](Hosting-the-relay.md)) and a name for this PC.
   - **Scan the code**: a QR code appears. It's valid for 5 minutes.
2. On the phone, tap **Add a PC** and scan the QR code. Confirm with your fingerprint.
3. **Compare codes**: both screens show a 6-digit code. It must be identical; this proves nobody
   swapped keys in between. Confirm on both.
4. **Recovery codes**: 10 codes are shown **once**. Save or print them and keep them away from the
   PC. Type one back to prove you kept them.
5. **Turn protection on.**

## Your first unlock

1. Press **Win+L**, then sign in with your password.
2. The PC shows *Approve on your phone* and a 2-digit number.
3. On the phone, **type that number** and confirm with your fingerprint. The desktop opens.

If you didn't start the sign-in, tap **Deny** or **This wasn't me**.
